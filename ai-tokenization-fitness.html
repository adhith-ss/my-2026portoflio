AI Tokenization Fitness

The Problem

Imagine that an individual is independently developing an online product in the current world of product development with AI as a co-creator. They realize that the number of tokens consumed varies by case, which puts them under pressure to work with a limited number of tokens or to reduce the token usage in the future through various strategies. This results in an unavoidable strain on their mental and physical health.

Idea

To tackle this, I came up with a quick thought of combining token consumption to motivate individuals by fitness through existing apple fitness application. I set a sample parameter of 1000 steps for every 10k tokens consumed or 5 extra reps in weight training for every 10k tokens. This activity can add on top of existing goal or utilized as a goal by itself. With foundational reliance on negative reinforcement principle, the individual can be aware of their activities run on AI media that directly translates into a positive impact in changing their fitness behavior due to excessive AI usage where it may seem as a gentle punishment but in a way it gives the individual to be accountable.

Function

The individual can connect their AI tool through a companion integration on Apple Fitness iOS app that pulls real token usage and pushes it to the watch. UsageAPIClient calls the provider's org usage API in intervals and sums the tokens.
