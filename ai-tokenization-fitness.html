AI Tokenization Fitness

The Problem

Imagine an individual developing an online product with AI as co-creator faces varying token usage over the time, which puts them under pressure to work with a limited number of tokens or to reduce the token usage in the future through various strategies. This action could cause mental and physical strain.

Idea

I proposed linking token use to fitness via Apple Fitness: I set a sample parameter of 1000 steps for every 10k tokens or 5 extra reps in weight training for every 10k tokens consumed. This can supplement or be a standalone goal. Using negative reinforcement principle, AI media activity encourages accountability and positive fitness behavior change.

Function

The individual can connect their AI tool through a companion integration on Apple Fitness iOS app that pulls real token usage and pushes it to the watch. UsageAPIClient calls the provider's org usage API in intervals and sums the tokens.
