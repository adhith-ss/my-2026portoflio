Rx - Delivery Order Fulfillment

Role

Senior UX Designer

Platform

Mobile-Web

Duration

10 months

Sam's Club - Walmart

Nearly half a million Sam's Club members benefit through the delivery order fulfillment, where thousands of pharmacy associates work through multiple process in receiving, validating, bagging and dispatching the orders at the stores. I was sole designer responsible for the complete discovery to end product delivery, which was executed in collaboration with 15 engineers and one product owner.

Result

The delivery fulfillment application redefined the member - associate experience across 5 pilot states.

55%

reduction in associate delivery dispense time

800+

orders fulfilled

Context

Growing demand for delivery orders and operational challenges forced Sam's Club Health & Wellness team to rethink the delivery order fulfillment process through an application called RxToGo.

The RxToGo application is a newer version of the Walmart-Sam's Club's legacy application: Connexus

The Previous Platform

Connexus is a legacy web application used by pharmacies across Sam's Club and Walmart. All dispense types are processed through this platform, and with respect to delivery - GoLocal is third party platform for performing delivery orders through Connexus.

GoLocal is used by associates to manually schedule delivery date and time

Through the Connexus application, a pharmacist will verify the prescription details with doctor's office and stage for medication fill, followed by:

  1. A pharmacy technician will scan the prescription, and process payment on the in-store POS device by contacting customer.
  2. Post payment the order will be scheduled upon available delivery time.
  3. On the last leg, they will print the documents and stage for delivery driver pickup.

Connexus - The Pharmacy Command Centre

Pharmacists and technicians utilize this web application to view all the pharmacy fulfillment orders

Problems identified

I spent first few weeks in learning about the problem space. I analyzed the end-to-end journey of order processing by pharmacy associates on both the physical and digital ends.

Associates said the process is tedious and delays in-store customer service.

Strenuous Navigation

The Connexus portal carries a heavy cognitive load making associates interact with multiple sections with prescription and patient details. No room for error is acceptable when dealing with sensitive information.

Laborious Task

The overall process requires associates to move back and forth between main work-desk to staging areas with interactions of scanners, computers, printers, telephones and hand-held devices.

Design Approach

After identifying problems with the overall fulfillment process, I outlined three areas for a new design to innovate in:

  1. Transparency — Bringing all the delivery order information upfront in a single dashboard.
  2. Integrated — Combine the delivery scheduling and checkout into a unified flow.
  3. Acquaintance — The tone and design system of the app is reflected around working language familiarity.

As pharmacy associates, they need accessible delivery fulfillment to efficiently complete orders and spend more time with customers.

Order Maintenance Dashboard

I simplified prescription order management with a clear dashboard to view orders and delivery updates.

Order Maintenance Dashboard

A linear action navbar makes it easier to navigate orders in queue and their statuses. The table is laid out by Name, Rx Count, Cost, Status, Bag and Origination, giving associates ample information to process an order, with all tertiary actions related to tech support kept accessible.

Processing Delivery Order

To streamline order processing, the previous hardware-software interactions were translated into a single linear layout for quick and easier processing.

Mobile Checkout

Post delivery scheduling, there is a need for scanning the prescription for checkout. The scanning and printing tasks are performed through this mobile app that reduces mobility and the POS dependancy.

Mobile Checkout

The prescription is prioritized for validation by pharmacists and doctors, with updates possible in one place without external navigation. Color coded bags identify prescriptions in staging when ready to checkout, payments can be viewed and updated without POS interaction, and the schedule is split by date and time based on GoLocal driver data, showing upfront available times to communicate with customers.

Systems Thinking

A mobile checkout product diversification was needed to perform the scanning and printing tasks that bypass the POS interaction and reduce associate mobility within the store.

1 · Leveraging An Existing App

A Walmart fulfillment app was redefined for order checkout to reduce engineering effort and ease user adaptation.

2 · Designing For Familiarity

Using a cohesive design system between Walmart and Sam's Club DLS, the app was designed around a unified brand guideline.

Usability Testing

I brought back the apps to validate with pharmacy associates using an interactive prototype.

Associates appreciated the web portal for delivery orders, reducing clicks and dependency on GoLocal.

The mobile app is more handy for checkout and finish tasks promptly.

Strengths

  • Skimming order details for a customer is easy with a linear step by step process.
  • Pre and post checkout details were clear.
  • The checkout app required less learning curve.

Opportunities

  • Reiterate content flow on the web app order queue with concise actionable items.
  • Refine the checkout order list on mobile app.
  • Hardware interruptions use case required attention.

Launch Ready-Design

Next Steps

Collect post-launch user feedback

Although many possible use cases were captured and rolled out in production, certain use cases arise that requires operational alternative or rethink product strategies. Likewise as a team, we had to wait on a periodic feedback collection for next steps.

Expand further on more fulfillment options

This new delivery fulfillment process gained confidence among business and pharmacy associates to encourage designing for other fulfillment options that can be integrated into this app.
